Graphic Designing

Course details

This 2-month course takes you from design basics to creating professional-looking graphics. You will learn how layout, color and typography work together, then apply them in Photoshop, Illustrator and Canva on real projects.

By the end you will be able to design logos, social media posts, banners, flyers and business cards, and present them in a portfolio you can show to clients or employers.

What you will be able to do

  • Design logos and brand kits
  • Create social media graphics
  • Edit and retouch photos
  • Prepare files for print and web

Who this course is for

  • Beginners with no design experience
  • Students who want to start freelancing
  • Business owners who want to design their own branding

2-month course outline

Month 1

  • Design fundamentals: layout, color theory, typography
  • Adobe Photoshop: layers, selections, masks, photo editing
  • Social media posts and ad creatives
  • Canva for quick, clean designs
  • Exporting files for web and print

Month 2

  • Adobe Illustrator: vector shapes and the pen tool
  • Logo design and brand identity basics
  • Flyers, banners, posters and business cards
  • Working with client briefs and revisions
  • Final portfolio project

Tools you will use

Adobe PhotoshopAdobe IllustratorCanva
