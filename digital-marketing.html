Course details

This 2-month course covers the main parts of digital marketing: search engine optimization, social media, paid advertising and analytics. Every topic is taught with practical exercises so you learn how campaigns are actually planned and measured.

You will finish with a campaign project that shows how you plan, run and report on marketing for a business.

What you will be able to do

  • Plan a social media content calendar
  • Run basic paid ad campaigns
  • Apply SEO basics to a website
  • Read analytics and report results

Who this course is for

  • Beginners who want a career in marketing
  • Business owners who want to promote their own business
  • Freelancers adding marketing to their services

2-month course outline

Month 1

  • Digital marketing fundamentals and customer journey
  • Content planning and social media strategy
  • Facebook and Instagram page setup and growth
  • SEO basics: keywords, on-page SEO, site structure
  • Copywriting for posts and ads

Month 2

  • Meta Ads Manager: campaigns, audiences, budgets
  • Google Ads introduction
  • Email marketing basics
  • Analytics and campaign reporting
  • Final campaign project

Tools you will use

Meta Ads ManagerGoogle AdsGoogle Analytics
