About EEIT

We prepare engineers who can think critically and build things that work.

The Empirical Engineering Institute of Technology (EEIT) grounds engineering education in experience, analysis and practical work. "Empirical" means learning through observation and experiment, and that is our guiding principle.

Through lab work, projects and industry-style problems, students are prepared to move from the classroom straight into meaningful work.

Our Mission

  • Practical, project-based education
  • Training in modern technology
  • Encouraging research and innovation
  • Career-ready graduates
