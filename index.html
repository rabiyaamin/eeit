About EEIT

We prepare engineers and IT professionals who can think critically and build things that work.

The Empirical Engineering Institute of Technology (EEIT) grounds education in experience, analysis and practical work. "Empirical" means learning through observation and experiment, and that is our guiding principle.

Through lab work, projects and industry-style problems, students are prepared to move from the classroom straight into meaningful work.

Our Mission

  • Practical, project-based education
  • Training in modern technology
  • Encouraging research and innovation
  • Career-ready graduates

Rabiya Amin

Chief Executive Officer, EEIT  |  MBA (Human Resources)

Rabiya Amin holds an MBA in Human Resources and brings that people-focused management background to technology education. She leads EEIT with a clear technology-first vision: every student should leave with skills they can demonstrate, not just certificates they can show. She drives the institute's IT direction, from the tools taught in class to the way each course is structured around real, working projects.

Under her leadership, the curriculum is built on the same platforms and workflows used in the industry today: design software for branding and digital media, e-commerce platforms for running online stores, analytics and advertising tools for digital marketing, and productivity automation for everyday office work.

Her approach follows the EEIT principle of empirical learning: test an idea, measure the result, improve it.
