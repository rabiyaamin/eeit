Course details

This 2-month course builds the computer skills used in almost every office job. You will learn to manage files and email, write professional documents, work with data in spreadsheets and create clear presentations.

The course is hands-on from the first class, with exercises based on real office tasks.

What you will be able to do

  • Create professional documents
  • Work confidently in Excel
  • Design clear presentations
  • Handle everyday office tasks efficiently

Who this course is for

  • Students and job seekers
  • Beginners new to computers
  • Office staff who want to work faster

2-month course outline

Month 1

  • Windows, file management and typing practice
  • Microsoft Word: formatting, tables, styles, mail merge
  • Email, internet and online tools
  • Document design for letters, reports and CVs
  • Printing and PDF basics

Month 2

  • Microsoft Excel: formulas, functions, sorting, filtering
  • Charts, pivot tables and data presentation
  • Microsoft PowerPoint: slides, design, animations
  • Productivity tips and shortcuts
  • Final office project

Tools you will use

Microsoft WordMicrosoft ExcelMicrosoft PowerPoint
