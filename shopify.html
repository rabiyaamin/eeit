Course details

This 2-month course teaches you how to start and run an online store on Shopify. You will learn how to research products, set up a store, add products and take payments, then promote the store and track results.

You will work on a live store project during the course, so you finish with practical experience instead of only theory.

What you will be able to do

  • Set up a complete Shopify store
  • Manage products, orders and customers
  • Connect payments and shipping
  • Promote and track store performance

Who this course is for

  • Beginners who want to start an online business
  • Freelancers who want to offer store setup services
  • Sellers moving from social media to their own store

2-month course outline

Month 1

  • E-commerce basics and how online selling works
  • Product and market research
  • Creating a Shopify store and choosing a theme
  • Adding products, collections and pages
  • Store design: homepage, menus, branding

Month 2

  • Payment gateways, shipping and taxes
  • Apps and store automation
  • Basic SEO and social media promotion
  • Orders, customers and analytics
  • Launch project: your live store
